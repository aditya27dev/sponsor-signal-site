Sponsor Signal

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Sponsor Signal does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. No accounts, analytics, advertising or third parties.

What it reads

On job pages (LinkedIn, Indeed, the job boards listed in the Chrome Web Store description, and, only if you turn it on, other sites that publish job data), the extension reads the employer name, advertised salary and job description. This happens only in your browser, to show the sponsor badge. It does not read your profile, messages or browsing history.

What it sends

Nothing about you or the pages you view leaves your browser. Once a day the extension downloads the public Register of licensed sponsors from GOV.UK (www.gov.uk and assets.publishing.service.gov.uk). These requests contain no information about you beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address, which GOV.UK may log under its own privacy notice.

What it stores

A copy of the public sponsor register, kept in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) so lookups work offline. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.
