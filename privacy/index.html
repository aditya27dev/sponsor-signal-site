Sponsor Signal

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Sponsor Signal reads job pages to show its sponsor badge. Everything happens on your device: nothing about you or the pages you view is sent to us or to anyone else. No accounts, analytics, advertising or third parties.

What it reads (website content)

On job pages (LinkedIn, Indeed, the job boards listed in the Chrome Web Store description, and, only if you turn it on, other sites that publish job data), the extension reads the employer name, advertised salary and job description. It does not read your profile, messages, form entries or browsing history.

How it uses it

Only to show the badge: it looks up the employer in the Home Office register, checks the salary against the Skilled Worker thresholds, and scans the description for wording about visa sponsorship or recruitment agencies. All of this runs locally in your browser.

What it keeps

Job page content is not saved. Recent employer lookups are kept in memory to avoid repeating them, and are cleared when the browser closes. The extension also stores a copy of the public sponsor register in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) so lookups work offline. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

What it sends

Nothing about you or the pages you view. Once a day the extension downloads the public Register of licensed sponsors from GOV.UK (www.gov.uk and assets.publishing.service.gov.uk). These requests contain no information about you beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address, which GOV.UK may log under its own privacy notice.

Selling and sharing

Data is never sold, transferred, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Limited Use

Sponsor Signal's use of information it reads from web pages complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
